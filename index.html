Frequently Answered

Clarifying Key Engagement Questions

Replacing heavy walls of text with direct answers to your team’s most frequent operational inquiries.

How fast can Hirexa deploy an engineering squad for a critical project?
For individual contract engineers and tech specialists, candidate shortlists are delivered within 48 hours, with onboarding completed in under 5 business days. For full GCC innovation centers or custom software teams, our 60-day Build-Operate-Transfer roadmap guarantees full deployment within 8 weeks.
Who owns the Intellectual Property (IP) and code developed by Hirexa teams?
100% of all intellectual property, source code, patents, and work products are assigned directly to your organization from Day 1. In our GCC Build-Operate-Transfer model, 100% of local entity equity and physical assets are transferred to you at the milestone date.
How does Hirexa manage international payroll and compliance across multiple countries?
Hirexa maintains registered corporate entities across the United States, United Kingdom, Poland, Germany, and India. We act as the Employer of Record (EOR), handling statutory benefits, local taxation, cross-border mobility visas, and currency conversion without exposing your firm to permanent establishment risks.
What is the difference between Hirexa Staffing and Hirexa RPO?
Staffing fulfills immediate tactical hiring requisitions (1–5 roles) with pre-screened talent. Recruitment Process Outsourcing (RPO) embeds an entire dedicated talent acquisition department into your company to manage all hiring from job marketing to onboarding, ideal for organizations hiring 25+ engineers per year.
